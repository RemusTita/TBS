# Taxi Booking System

The Taxi Booking System is meant to enhance administrator, driver, and passenger experiences. Consumers can register, view their booking history, cancel bookings, sign up and schedule taxis depending on pickup and drop-off points, dates, and times. Drivers will log in to verify they follow the schedule and complete their booked trips. Drivers assigned to client appointments will check for scheduling conflicts including overlapping trips under the management of administrators.
The program's GUI allows users to interact with it easily. Client data, booking records and driver assignments will all be securely stored in the database and accessed upon program restarts.
